02 · Why us

Why work with Petalxtech?

Not a vendor you brief and forget. A team that stays with the system it built, and tells you the truth about it.

Small by design

Staying small is what lets the person who designed your system still be the person answering questions about it three years later. You will know our names, and we will know your codebase.

English, end to end

Calls, documents, code review and commit messages: all of it in English, by the same engineers who write the code. Nothing is lost on the way to us, and nothing needs translating on the way back.

Prevention, not triage

We treat a defect as something to prevent rather than something to catch. It costs a little more in the first month and a great deal less every year after — the part most vendors never have to live with, because they are gone by then.

03 · Approach

Substance over noise

Three steps, no theatre. You see something real early, and you always know what it cost.

  1. 1

    Understand the system you have

    Before proposing anything we read the code and talk to the people running it. You get a straight account of what to keep, what to replace, and what each will cost — including when the honest answer is that you need less than you thought.

  2. 2

    Build the smallest thing that works

    Shipped and running beats designed and pending. We put something real in front of you early, watch how it behaves with your data and your people, and grow it from there.

  3. 3

    Stay after launch

    Day two is the part most vendors leave. Operating what we built is how we find out whether we built it properly, and it is where a system either earns its keep or quietly starts costing you.

05 · Company

A team hired anywhere, building for clients everywhere

We began with servers, code, and the ordinary work of keeping systems running. That is still exactly what we do. What we added was the other half of the problem: companies on the far side of the world who need software built properly, and a team that builds it that way.

Our clients are in the United States and beyond. Our engineers are wherever we find them: the company is rooted in Tsuwano, Shimane, and the team is not limited to it. What holds the work together is a shared working language, and a way of building that treats a defect as something to prevent rather than triage.

We are a small company by design, and we are taking on new clients. If you have a system that needs building, rebuilding, or simply keeping alive, we would like to hear about it.

06 · FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What people ask us before the first call.

How do we start working together?

Send the problem through the form, or take a thirty-minute slot in the calendar — whichever suits you. The first conversation is with an engineer rather than a salesperson, and its only purpose is to work out whether we are the right people for the job. If we are not, we will say so.

Can you work alongside our existing engineering team?

Yes, and often that is the better shape. We join your repositories, your review process and your standups, and work as part of the team rather than a separate island that hands over a zip file at the end.

Can the whole engagement run in English?

Yes. Calls, documentation, code review and commit messages are all in English, handled by the engineers doing the work rather than by an account manager relaying messages.
