Who we are

We connect Japanese craft with global ambition

We began with servers, code, and the ordinary work of keeping systems running. Technology is still at our core, but it was never the point. What we actually do is join two things that rarely meet: the discipline of Japanese engineering, and companies on the other side of the world who need software built properly.

Our clients are mainly in the United States. Our engineers are in Shimane. Between them sits a working day that begins as America’s ends, and a way of building that treats a defect as something to prevent rather than triage.

We are a small company by design. Staying small is what lets the person who designed your system still be the person answering questions about it three years later.

What we believe

Skill comes from many places

Engineers, designers and testers. And equally the people who keep the office running, who know the town, who train the next intake. Each carries something the work needs.

Cooperation creates value

Opportunity reveals potential

Our mission

Helping people and companies use everything they have

Two things drive this company, and they are the same thing seen from two directions.

  • Develop the potential already in the room

    People arrive able to do more than their title says. Most of our senior engineers were hired before they looked senior on paper.

  • Join technology to the people who live with it

    A system is only finished when the people who depend on it can run it without us in the room.

  • Leave lasting value in the region

    Not only in the product. A town this size feels every job that stays in it.

Whether you are a business owner, an engineer, a researcher, or someone ready to find out what you are capable of, PetalX is open to working together.

Together, we build things that last.
