02 · Why us

Why work with PetalX?

Not a vendor you brief and forget. A team that stays with the system it built.

Small by design

Staying small is what lets the person who designed your system still be the person answering questions about it three years later.

Your evening, our morning

Our working day begins as America’s ends. Work handed over at close of business is underway before you are back at your desk.

Prevention, not triage

A way of building that treats a defect as something to prevent rather than something to catch. It costs more up front and less every year after.

03 · Approach

Substance over noise

  1. Understand the system you have

    Before proposing anything we read the code and talk to the people running it: what to keep, what to replace, and what each will cost.

  2. Build the smallest thing that works

    Shipped and running beats designed and pending. We put something real in front of you early, then grow it.

  3. Stay after launch

    Day two is the part most vendors leave. Operating what we built is how we find out whether we built it properly.

06 · Company

Engineers in Shimane, clients in the United States
