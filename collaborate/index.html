01 · What we collaborate on

Three ways we work together

Most partnerships start with one of these. They tend not to stay there.

Development

Backend, web, mobile, cloud and AI work on live client systems — reviewed by the engineers who will keep running it.

Remote roles

Ongoing remote positions on engagements we already hold, worked from wherever you live.

Client projects

Delivery alongside us on named client work, from first scope through to day-two operation.

02 · How it works

Three steps, and none of them is an interview panel

We read your public work before we ever ask you for a CV.

  1. 1

    A thirty-minute call

    We look at what you have built — GitHub, a portfolio, anything you point us at — and then we talk. The call is with an engineer, not a recruiter.

  2. 2

    A first project, paid

    We start with something small and real. Both sides find out how the other works before anyone commits to more.

  3. 3

    It grows from there

    As the work proves out, the scope widens — and so does what it returns, month by month. For people who want one, it can become a full-time seat on the team.

03 · What you get

What a partnership is worth

Revenue that grows with the work

Start with one project. As engagements widen, so does what they return each month — this is built to compound rather than to stay flat.

A route to full-time

Collaboration can turn into a permanent seat for anyone who wants one. Plenty of people would rather it did not, and that is fine too.

English, end to end

Calls, documents, code review and commit messages, all in English, with the engineers doing the work rather than a manager relaying it.

Work you can put your name to

Reviewed, tested, and built to last — the same standard we hold our own code to. Nothing you would be embarrassed to show later.

04 · Questions

Before you write to us

Do I need to move, or work Japanese hours?

No. Every collaboration is remote, and you work from where you already are.

What are you looking for?

Public work we can read, and evident care in how it is built. Years matter less than what your code says about you.

What if I only have a few hours a week?

Say so in your first message. Small engagements are how most of these start anyway.
